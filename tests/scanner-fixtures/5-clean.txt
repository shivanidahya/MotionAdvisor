Defendant moves to dismiss under Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(b)(6). The Massachusetts Superior Court and the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts require a complaint to state a plausible claim. See Bell Atlantic Corp. v. Twombly, 550 U.S. 544 (2007); Iannacchino v. Ford Motor Co., 451 Mass. 623 (2008). Under Mass. R. Civ. P. 12(b)(6) and Superior Court Rule 9A, the Court should allow the Motion.
